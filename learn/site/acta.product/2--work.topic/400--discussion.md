Use a task's **Activity** tab for discussion and progress. Task changes share
the feed with comments, so decisions remain beside the work they explain.

## Post or reply

1. Open the task and select **Activity**.
2. Write a comment, or select **Reply** on the comment you want to answer.
3. Select **Post** or **Reply**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>
  (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> on macOS).
4. Check that the posted content appears in the feed.

Replies remain in their original thread, including replies to other replies.
Unsent posts are retained in this browser for the account and task. Unsaved
comment edits are not retained after a reload.

## Edit with other contributors

Your comment permission allows editing your own comments. Managing someone
else's comments requires a separate permission. A conflicting edit preserves
your text and presents the latest saved content for reconciliation.

Deleting a comment removes its text and leaves a placeholder for replies. Acta
does not keep full comment revision history or provide comment restoration.

## Read activity

Consecutive edits by the same actor can appear as one activity entry.
Description changes record that an edit happened; the activity feed is not a
backup of previous descriptions.

Acta marks visible activity read while the page is focused. Collapsed replies
remain unread, and command-line or MCP reads do not acknowledge the browser's
activity. An edit after acknowledgment can make an entry unread again.
