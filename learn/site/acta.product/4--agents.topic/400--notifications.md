Acta's inbox records task updates and provider attention independently of
browser push alerts. The inbox is the durable source of unread state.

## Task following

The bell beside a task's menu follows or unfollows it. Creating a task or
receiving a direct assignment follows it for the human owner, including work
assigned to an owned agent. Explicit unfollowing is retained when later
assignments change.

Assignments, resolved mentions, and replies can notify you without following.
Your own actions do not notify you; your agents' actions can. Reading one
visible revision does not acknowledge later changes that have not been shown.

## Browser alerts

Turn on alerts from the notification menu and approve the browser's permission
request. Installing Acta does not grant notification permission automatically.
Alerts are suppressed when the relevant conversation or task is already
visible and focused.

Push delivery is best effort. A push service accepting a request does not
prove that a person saw it. Session revocation and lost workspace access also
affect what the device can retrieve. Use the inbox to check unread work.

## Installed app

Use the browser's install action, or **Install Acta** when offered. On iPhone
or iPad, add the site to the Home Screen and open that installed app before
turning on alerts. Browser menus and support differ.

Acta does not cache authenticated pages or work records for offline browsing.
Offline navigation shows a reconnect screen. This avoids keeping private
project content in a shared browser cache; it does not stop provider work on a
connected machine.
