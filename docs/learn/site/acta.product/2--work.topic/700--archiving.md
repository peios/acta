---
title: "Archiving Work"
type: how-to
description: >-
  Put away a task subtree and restore it without losing its history.
---

Archive work when you want to remove it from active views while retaining its
content and history. Archiving is independent of completion and does not
delete anything.

## Archive a task

1. Open the task and check its subtasks.
2. In the task actions menu, select **Archive task**.
3. Check the **Archived** banner and the workspace's **Archived tasks** view.

You need **Edit tasks**. The operation archives the task and its active
descendants together. Archived tasks allow reading, downloads, following, and
read acknowledgments, but reject content changes.

## Restore a task

1. Open **Archived tasks**, or follow the task's direct link.
2. If its parent is archived, restore the ancestor first.
3. Select **Restore** and check the task in its active board.

Restoration returns the descendants archived by that operation. A child
archived separately beforehand remains archived. Statuses, assignments,
documents, and parent relationships are preserved.

Ordinary search excludes archived tasks. Select **Include archived** where
available when looking for earlier work.
