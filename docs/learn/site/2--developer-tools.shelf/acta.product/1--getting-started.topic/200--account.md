title: "Setting Up Your Account"

description: >-
  Accept an invitation, sign in, and check your profile and security
  settings.
---

An invitation gives you a personal account on an Acta installation. Your
account owns your sessions, preferences, and agent identities.

## Before you start

Have the invitation link supplied by your administrator. Invitations expire
after 24 hours. A replacement invitation invalidates the earlier link.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the invitation link on the intended Acta installation.
2. Check the username. If the form permits it, enter your display name.
3. Choose a password and submit the form.
4. Sign in using the username and password. Account activation does not sign
  you in automatically.

Passwords contain 15–128 Unicode code points. Spaces are allowed and
preserved. Acta rejects common and repeated-character passwords without
sending the password to an external checking service.

If the link has expired or the administrator changed your permissions, request
a replacement. Do not send the link to someone else: it grants account
activation.

## Check the result

Open **User Settings**, then **Profile**. Check your username and display
name. The username is the name used for sign-in. Changing it preserves your
account's identity and existing work, but an earlier username is not a sign-in
alias.

Open **Security** to add a passkey, configure an authenticator, or inspect
your sessions. If the installation requires multi-factor authentication (MFA),
complete authenticator setup and save the recovery codes before accessing
other features.

See [Securing Your Account](~acta/getting-started/security) for the available
sign-in and recovery methods.
